# Chapter 4 \- ducks

“Well, fuck” they both said at the same time.


The three beetles were already taking their slow, skittering steps towards them. Cas whipped out his pistol and pulled the trigger. Instead of the bright red laser lancing out of the barrel, the large ring gave off a limp glow of reddish orange, as if it was a recently dead light-bulb that was turned on and simply gave up in the process. Shit.


“Sawyer! Did you get it working yet?” he called over his shoulder.


“No! How the hell do we fight those things?” the boy called in response.


“Well, I guess the answer is the hard way then! Do you think you can take one?”


“You're kidding me right?!” came the panicked reply.


“Crap, ok, I’m out of shots, can you try and charge this up to full as fast as you can?” Cas tossed his pistol over to Sawyer, who caught it with surprising deftness and gave him a nod. “Don’t bother with just a charge or two, I’m pretty sure I’ll need all three to take down one of these things!” They had figured out that you could get the winding time down by at least 15 seconds if you cranked it really hard, but it took enough effort that you tired out before you got through the second shot. He would need to create at least three minutes of leeway for Sawyer to get the pistol charged up again. With it passed off and Sawyer cranking away, Cas pulled the machete out and charged the bugs.


They turned out to be far more aggressive than their "smaller" cousins. While the beetles had been attracted to Sawyer ever since they linked up, it was little more than the bugs pointing themselves towards them when they got close and wandering in their direction. Even as more and more popped up, it was little effort to take them out.


It was so easy In fact, that Cas had stopped getting experience from killing them maybe half an hour to an hour back, as if they were so menial to offer no benefit to killing them. As soon as he'd had that thought, an achievement window had popped up.


The system seemed to clearly want him to fight something more significant to actually complete the quest, but he hadn't seen anything other than the smaller beetles up until now.


These bugs however, were the real deal. Instead of just idly wandering towards them, they seemed to immediately turn towards them as soon as they entered the clearing and were still picking up pace.


Cas met them head on in the middle of the 40-or-so meter clearing, and took a tentative swipe with the machete at the head of the lead bug. The blade skated off the hard carapace of the head, leaving only a small gouge in the chitinous material.


The lead bug took advantage of the attack, surprising Cas by lunging forwards in a bite that nearly took his hand off at the wrist before he jerked it away. He back-pedaled to get some space between them before squaring up with it again. It didn't take long to realize his mistake.


By the time he had swung twice more, this time making sure to follow through and get his hand out of the reach of its jaws, his mistake became clear.  The other two had caught up and now spread out, as if to encircle him.


Luckily, it seemed they weren't that smart. They each spread out just enough to be able to snap at him and no further. The second beetle took a swipe at him with its large front claw and he just barely managed to get his makeshift shield up in time to deflect it. The shield came away intact but with a noticeable gouge across it. While their shells looked pretty muchthe same, these larger beetles seemed to have a harder carapace in addition to their size. Yaaay.


If his years of playing video games taught him anything, he knew this; if he was going to make it through this, he needed to do at least two things. One, he needed to wait for openings and counter, and two, he'd need to kill at least a couple of them without his gun. *Great, I can just pull on all the zero experience I have doing any of that in real life*, he thought to himself. It was time to kite.


He backpedaled, doing his best to keep the beetles from accidentally encircling him or pushing him into a corner with his back up against the dense wall of trees at the edges of the clearing. To make matters worse, he only had half the clearing to move around in. When he first tried to make some space, he'd lead them too close to Sawyer and one of them peeled off to try and take a bite out of him. That had led to a frantic little fracas as Sawyer ran away from the giant bug while Cas called at him to lead it back towards him. Luckily the plan worked, and the beetle's attention was immediately drawn back onto Cas when Sawyer got just a few meters away.


Their claws were a big problem too. Fortunately, he could pretty easily avoid their bites thanks to them having no necks, forcing them to lunge their whole torso forward every time they tried to bite him. Unfortunately,  they had much better reach with their claws, not to mention mobility. More times than not, when he tried to go for another swipe at their heads they had swung at him with their claws and he wasn't even able to get within range thanks to their superior reach. All the rapid lunging and dodging was really starting to tire him out. The claws had to go first.


He jogged back a few meters to gain some space and to isolate the lead beetle, and readied himself. Thankfully, they weren't super fast. but unlike the smaller beetles who could be outpaced at a casual walking speed, these ones were at least fast enough that he needed to make an effort.


With his shield at the ready, he feinted forwards just enough to be in the range of its left claw.  Relief flooded him when he saw the bug took the bait easily. It made an aggressive lunge towards him, snapping its mandible like a hungry beast and lashed our with a swipe of its claw,  but he side-stepped the overextended swing with ease before taking a swipe down at the less armoured upper joint of its leg.


The awkward angle of a left-handed swing at the creature's left leg weakened the hit somewhat, but still managed to bite deeply into the flesh of the limb. When it tried to put its leg back down, the limb gave out and it listed hard to the side, nearly toppling over. As it tried to steady itself, it planted its right front claw into the ground in front of it, which opened up the limb perfectly for another swipe downward.


This time, Cas had the angle and timing on his side, and his machete carried completely through the limb, fully separating it from the body. While unfortunately that did not cause the beetle to topple over fully, it did have to compensate for the missing front limbs and sit its weight back on its middle and rear legs.


This caused its head-stump to angle up towards him, and it clacked its vertical mandibles barely a foot away from his face. His heart pumping hard with exertion and adrenaline, Cas stepped back, just enough so that he could plunge the machete directly down into its open mouth. The beetle died instantly.


The sudden weight pulled Cas downwards as the massive bug slumped to the dirt and he nearly lost his grip on the machete before pulling it out at the last second purely in the reflex of trying to get away.


His victory was short lived however, as the two other beetles had closed the distance and left him with very little room to move. He tried to dodge through a gap to the left, and felt a searing pain high on his right arm as he passed. Taking a quick moment to look down as he made some distance again, he saw that one of the bugs had caught him with its claw and left a wide slash across his right arm.


"Gah!" he cried out in pain, "damn that hurts! Sawyer, hows the gun coming?" He called over.


"I'm almost done with the second charge!" Sawyer called back. "Are you ok?"


"Well, it hurts like a mother, but I don't think it’s too deep!" Cas replied, catching his breath as he finished crossing to the opposite side of the clearing from the bugs. *Only two?* It had felt like he had been fighting for at least 10 minutes, not just two.


But he had a strategy now. He took a couple more moments to catch his breath, and sprinted towards the remaining beetles who had already managed to close the distance again. He side-stepped just before he reached the small column of bugs causing the first bug's stabbing swipe to miss him by a wide margin. Their front claws had better reach, but a narrow angle they could swing at effectively.


As he passed by it, he slashed out at it, cleanly cutting off its back leg. The bug listed to the side and struggled to turn itself towards him. Instead of trying to finish it off though, he kept running, pulling the last bug further across the clearing and away from its friend.


As he once again made it to the far end of the clearing, Cas was breathing heavily. they may have fixed his legs, but he had spent the last few years in a wheelchair and working at a desk. Now that he had a clear plan and strategy in mind for how to deal with the bugs, the adrenaline had started to wear off and he could now feel the muscles of his freshly mended legs screaming at him in protest from all the sudden movement. Just a minute more and he could stop. He had to keep moving.


He shook his head clear and moved towards the oncoming beetle. Again, he sidestepped to the right, avoiding the forward swing of its claws. This time however, Cas didn't reserve himself to a single leg, and instead cut each of the legs on its right side off one at a time. in a few moments, the bug was topped over on its side and struggling fruitlessly to pull itself back up.


Cas quickly made his way around to the bug's left and rammed the machete up into its now-exposed underbelly. When it didn't stop squirming, he pulled the machete back out and plunged it down into the bug again right behind the joint for the front left leg, angling the blade towards the head.


That did the job, and the massive bug collapsed, falling down and and luckily pulling itself off his blade as it went. *Only one more to go.* He had to pull himself back hard to avoid being skewered by the last remaining beetle's claw as it swung at him from just out of the corner of his eye. Nearly falling over and catching himself on one knee, he got his shield up just in time to take the brunt of the second claw plunging towards him.


The weight from the hit knocked him fully onto his back and he rolled frantically to the side to get away from the bug's follow-up stabs. He pulled himself to his feet just in time to block another strike with his shield, but this time was able to parry it away into a glancing blow that didn't send him sprawling back to the forest floor.


He could feel his legs and arms about to give out, so in one last burst of movement, he took out the massive bug's other two right limbs, leaving it to crash to the side just like its fellow. Knowing where to aim this time, he put the tip of his machete right up in the beetle's armpit and drove the point down into whatever it used for a brain.


"Got it!" Sawyer called out, right as Cas collapsed to the dirt in exhaustion.


"Gods below, we've really been walking for five hours?? At least the quest is done though I guess?" Cas said as he accepted his pistol back from Sawyer and pulled himself up into a sitting position with not a small amount of their help.


"You completed a quest?" Sawyer asked.


"Yeah, the level up one, 'Suck Less' or whatever. Didn't you say you had it too?"


"Yeah, but I just got the notification that I hit level 4?" Sawyer replied as he suddenly produced a bandage roll and wrapped it around Cash’s arm. Apparently he’d got it in a **[ Baby’s first first-aid kit ]** reward box for not dying from eating the fruit, similar to how Cas had got the screwdriver.


"Huh, I guess you got credit for being in the fight, maybe because that one chased you? I guess we just need to find you something to kill so you can level up. My timer said we still had most of three days remaining." Cas said. "Wait..."


"What the hell?" Cas asked no one in particular."What's that supposed to mean?"


They both found out quickly though, because before he could even explain the pop-up to Sawyer, they heard heavy movement coming from the far side of the clearing.


Turning around they saw it. Crashing through the branches that made a wall around the clearing was a truly *massive* beetle. The last pack of beetles had been up to Cash's waist, this one however, was at his eye level, likely even more.


"Fuck. A. Duck."
