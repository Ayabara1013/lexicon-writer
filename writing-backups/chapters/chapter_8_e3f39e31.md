# Chapter ?8

(The morning after arriving in Neon)


** knock knock* *


“You ready to go?” Cas asked, opening the door to Sawyer’s unit. “*Hahaha* sorry to spook ya bud! Checkin’ yourself out there?” He apologized with a chuckle. As soon as he opened the door, Sawyer had let out a high pitched squeak and spun around to face him.


”Nice pose *haha* grab your shirt and let’s hit the Adventuring Guild Cosmo mentioned yeah?” Cas tossed him a cereal bar he’d grabbed from a vending machine down the hall, causing Sawyer to lower his hands from covering his chest to catch it awkwardly.


As they made their way down the elevator to the lobby, Cas asked Sawyer, “So is that what you borrowed the credits for? The haircut I mean?” Sawyer immediately got red around the ears at the question. “I’m not bothered, just curious!”


”Uuum,… not exactly…” Sawyer replied hesitantly.


Cas wasn’t sure what had the kid so nervous but he tried to comfort him. “It’s all good man, I’m not one to push, or judge for that matter! Besides, you saved my ass yesterday, you don’t even need to give me a reason!” He laughed, patting him on the shoulder. To Cas’s pleasant surprise, that elicited a small shy smile on the young man’s soft face.


A few minutes later they passed an orange neon sign naming a shop “Lead Addiction”. Hold up, let me grab something real quick.” Cas said and ran inside, coming out a couple minutes later with a glass and metal lump about the size of a kiwi. “Rechargeable mana cell. It’s a real basic one and I had to buy it from the second-hand bin, but it’ll let me use the gun without having to crank it up constantly. And apparently it can recharge from my own mana passively to store a handful of shots as well! Have you gotten used to the whole ‘having mana’ thing yet?”


”**Not. At. All.**” Sawyer laughed along with Cas as they walked down the street in search of the Adventurer’s Guild. “I mean, how would you feel if one day you could just,” Sawyer gestured in the air vaguely for a moment, “fucking… Magic! Pow!” he pushed his hands out as if he were casting a spell.


“Didn't get my Hogwarts letter or nuthin’! Got fuckin’ cheated I tell you…” Cas heard him mutter under his breath.


“Hey, at least you didn't wake up in nothing but your undies after being dropped a meter onto your no-longer-paralyzed tailbone, Now *that* was something I was definitely not expecting!” they continued to banter as they made their way through the city, following the flow of other players.
