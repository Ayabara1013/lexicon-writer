# Chapter 5 \- Bigger Ducks

"PLEASE TELL ME YOU'VE GOT A MASSIVE CAN OF BUG SPRAAAY!" Cas screamed out as he ran in circles around the large clearing.


*Aaah gad… * cough * cough ** *best Morgan Freeman voice here we go…* However, it was clear that neither of them, in fact, had a massive-ass can of bug spray.”


Jesus is all this kid can do just run back and forth? I know, right? Like, who wrote this?! Wait, who am I? Who do you think? I'm the dude telling the story! Come on, there's like a quintillion bajillion aliens out there or some other stupid number, watching the broadcast feed with their own sports commentator wannabe, why can't I do my thing? Hey, this is my story and I do what I want!


Alright, let's get back to this though, he was running around like an idiot, then getting shish-kabobed by the evil giant ladybug right? Yeah, just… there, got it, That flash-forward-then-back thing…


…


*It would be just his luck to die screaming about bug spray*, Cas thought to himself as his vision cleared just in time to see his death swinging down towards him. *You know what? I'd rather not watch as it turns out.* He thought, closing his eyes once more in the brief moments he had before the massive LEG SCYTHE pinned him to the ground.


***“GAAAAH!”***


The cry of surprise made him tentatively peel his eyes back open, surprised at his lack of not-having-been-mega-dead-already.


*Huh, would you look at that.*


*WAIT, WHAT THE FUCK?*


Pinning the hell-bug up in the air was a wall of massive *thorns*. There was no other way to put it. Extending out a good two meters from the ground at a 30 degree angle, and sporting a lovely cornucopia of additional baby thornsSPEARS along its length, the large green wall of lovely safe death spikes had evidently saved his life?


“I figured it out!” Sawyer’s voice called from behind him. “Aaah, you might want to move, it’s starting to wiggle itself free!” True to his word, the hell-bug was already rocking its suspended front half back and forth as it struggled to get at Cash, and seemed to already be a good ways towards dislodging itself. Cas quickly scrambled away, not even sparing the time to get up on two feet till he was well out from under the monster.


“How did you figure it out?!” Cas asked, panting with his hands on his knees as he reached the notably worse-for-wear young man. “Don’t get me wrong, I am thankful, VERY thankful, but also like, *HOW??*”


Sawyer responded through a lightheaded smile that managed to work its way through the pained winces and grunts. “So, the whole time, I’d been trying to use the thing like a magic wand right? Point it and cast a spell, yeah? But plant’s aren’t some kind of TV remote or a garage door opener!” He waved away Cash’s “no shit Sherlock” facial expression and continued. “The description said to use my will, and look at my back!” He turned slightly and Cas leaned to see not a single hole in his shirt or skin where the thorn bushes should have riddled him like Swiss cheese.


“I *willed* it to happen \- no,  Seriously!” He added after a quizzical look from Cash. “From the moment I saw the leg swinging towards me, I just squeezed my eyes tight and thought; *please don't hurt me, please don't hurt me, please don't hurt me*, and they didn't. I checked when I got up, and the thorns had flattened themselves over to cushion my fall. Don't get me wrong, when I tried to use the staff in that last moment to protect you, it was still pretty startling!”


Cas could tell Sawyer would keep going on about it in his lightheaded adrenaline high, and he was almost about to let him but that's when they both heard the loud ***CRUNCH*** from behind. The damn hell bug had crushed \- actually *crushed* \- the thorns, and was now working its way towards them once more, completely ignoring the small collection of little green spears still lodged in its underbelly where they’d been broken off.


”Shit, think you can repeat that feat?” Cas asked as his panic fueled adrenaline began to refuel itself.


“I can certainly try!” Sawyer replied, thrusting the head of his staff towards the bug. A single large thorn shot out of the ground in front of the bug at a slight angle, knocking it slightly off balance, but not stopping its advance.


“Keep it up!” Cas said, trying to get himself into a ready stance.


Sawyer’s eyes narrowed in focus, and a new thorn erupted from the ground every half-second or so. Each thorn managed to knock it off balance just slightly and slow its advance, but it was clear that the thorns were weakening. They slowed to one every second, then every other second, and each was smaller than the last. Within six thorns, they had already stopped.


”Can you hit it again?” Cas asked.


“I’m not sure,” Sawyer replied as he extended the staff and focused once again and in seconds, the thorns erupted again. They kept up like that, slowly backpedaling as the thorns kept the monster at bay, but it was clear that while the thorns were slowing it, they were doing negligible damage that either of them could see aside from superficial surface scratches in the chitin.


“Damnit! Why can’t I get that big-ass one again like the first time!?” Sawyer cursed as he cast the thorns for the tenth time. “Come-on, ‘*Big-ass-thorn’*, ‘*spikey-death-punch’*, ‘*Fuck-yourself-off-with-some-Megafloura-jutsu*’! ***[Thorn Strike]***!” He shouted out phrases as if he was a character in some ninja or wizard anime, in vain hope that it would somehow trigger these ‘magic powers’. It was the last one that did it. As he shouted *Thorn Strike*, a massive green thorn erupted from the ground and pierced once again into the monster.


“There you go!” Cas cheered. Skewering right into the thick flesh of the upper ‘thigh’ of one of its large fore-legs, causing a steady trail of orange mucousy fluid to drip its way from the wound down the length of the thorn.


“KRREEEEAAAAAYYYAAEEEEEEEEEEEEAAAEAAAAAA!!!!!”


The beast let out a massive screech of either pain or indignation and began to struggle to get itself free, getting more agitated than they had yet seen it. Covering their ears from the piercing insectoid wail, Cas and Sawyer took the opportunity to make some distance between them and the beast even as the thorn spike began to crack under its thrashing weight.


”oh fuck it.” Cas said, closing one eye and aiming for the beast’s back legs and firing off a shot of blazing light from his pistol. The shot missed the back leg he had aimed for but it did manage to sear into the fleshy joint of the mid-leg next to it, and caused the clattering beast to slow and stumble noticeably for a moment.


“Gods dammit! Shit, ok, can you keep up that cantrip spell whatever for a bit? I need to recharge that shot!” Cas asked Sawyer after cursing his missed shot.


“Uuuuum, what's a cantrip? Like some tripping spell or something?” Sawyer replied.


“Your cantrip! Like in D\&D or something… fuck me, you've never played D\&D have you? World of Warcraft?” With each sentence, Cas was greeted by a confused look and returned the kid an even more confused look. This whole freaking scenario was just ridiculous. They’d been abducted by some sort of super powerful alien species, put in some magical pocket dimension or on some planet, given *some sort of weird experience portsof all things*? and pushed to murder a bunch of random bugs, and now he was in the middle of a freakin’ boss battle with the pillbug from hell, some giant spiky and jagged monstrosity the size of a damn minivan! It was bizzare enough he couldn't help but laugh out loud.


“The spell! it seems repeatable, can you keep it up for a minute or so?”


”I think so! I can kinda feel the drain, but I’d say I’m still at 60 percent or so!”


“Good! I would *really* not like to be this thing’s version of meow mix!”


***


After ninety seconds that felt like just as many minutes, Cas was breathing heavily and wheezing from a pair of new slashes across his arm and calf, but he was nearly ready.


“***GyaaaH!*** I think that's all I got!” Sawyer called out as he finished gathering power and sent a large wall of thorns erupting out of the ground between them and the monster.


“**Damn**! And all you got?” Cas asked for clarification.


“I kinda had to cludge it together so it burned a HUGE amount of my mana. I sorta figured out I can “cook” the spell to prep extra spikes or whatever, but I think I overcooked it there.”


“Cook?”


“Yeah, like a grenade?” Sawyer replied as he bent over panting, hands on his knees from the exhertion.


“So you know *Call of Duty* terms but not even Dungeons and Dragons?” Cas replied, shaking his head.


“CoD? Eww naw, my dad was a huge WW2 buff because his dad Served in Korea and *his* was in Patton's Africa Corps, so I heard all about it since I was a baby.” Sawyer replied defensively, seemingly very offended at the suggestion he played a game like that. “Besides, my brothers never let me play with them..”


“Ok, I'll give it to you, that's pretty cool. My grandfather FLEW lancasters in the RAF, back in…” ***CRASH!!***


A massive crash and a keening crunch of wood came from the other side of the barrier, cutting off their tangent.


“Shit, how long can this hold?” Cas asked Sawyer as his adrenaline spiked right back to the redline.


“We should be good for a little bit, that took nearly half of my total mana I think. It's got to be thick, right?!”


Before Cas could reply, the monster slammed into the wall again, hard enough to cause half of the thorns to crack, and one to break off and fall to the ground towards them. As they watched, the bug reached a massive claw through the gap and pulled.


Starting with a loud snapping and crunching noise, one by one the thorns buckled and snapped, sending wooden fibers showering towards the terrified pair. Once it cleared a gap roughly a meter and a half wide it leaned its head forward and started crunching down on the fibrous bulk of the thicker bases. It sawed down vigorously with its mandibles onto the thicker segments, clearing its newly made hole wider and wider with each bite.


“Fuuuck fuuuuuck! I can't do shit!” Cas cussed, and he wasn't wrong. His index finger twitched on the trigger of his blaster, but all he could see was its extremely dense carapace, well proved to be immune to any shots he could fire.


“Dammit! I still can’t do anything!” Sawyer added. “I think that last one burned me out!”


*Shit.* He really had no options left to him. Cas tried slashing at it, knowing that his machete would do little more than cosmetic damage to the steel-like sheel. The beast didn't even acknowledge the strikes as it kept chewing. He tried slashing at its mandibles as it chewed. The attacks caused little noticeable damage, but it seemed to anger the monster as it paused briefly to raise its “face” and let out a bone-chilling screech at him before snapping out with its mandibles to try and bite them.


Leaping back just barely in time, Cas saw a small gap between the mandibles and its upper “dome” of carapace where its face should otherwise be. He didn’t dare try and go for the soft interior of its mouth, but he thought he might be able to get his machete in there without losing his hand. He stabbed forward.


Cas could feel just a slight bit of resistance as the edge of blade skated and deflected off the two opposing pieces of carapace. The blade made its way maybe eight inches into whatever flesh was inside before the beast let out another of its deafening screeched and wrenched itself away. Cas briefly tried to keep hold of his machete, but he could have just as easily stopped a moving car. The hilt was pulled from his hand and the blade bent at an awkward angle as the beetle pulled up and away.


The beetle now reared up and tried to slam down onto the widened section of the thorn wall, but wasn’t able to make a significant dent in the lower portions of their thick trunks. After a couple of attempts, it surprised them both by walking backwards. It kept going all the way to the far side of the clearing, and Cas suddenly knew what it was about to do.


He looked down at his now fully charged and angrily glowing blaster and knew he had only one choice. ”Oh dammit,” he said to himself as he took a couple steps back and bounced on the balls of his feet.


***


Sawyer was utterly terrified and had absolutely no clue what Cas was thinking of doing when he heard the man mutter barely audibly under his breath, “[Glory, glory, what a hell of a way to die,](https://youtube.com/shorts/b9hRCnTUbQc?si=eaDfaCAFoetIX0R-)” before launching himself forward towards the now charging hell bug like a professional sprinter with a death wish. The tall, pale-haired man made it to the hole in the barricade in just a few strides and proceeded to leap forwards into the air, ***vaulting*** himself over the gap with his spare hand in one smooth movement.


***


Despite the bug’s massive stride churning up the dirt beneath it, Cas’s adrenaline somehow took him at least half of the way across the clearing just as fast. All this had been such a blur that the thought of having to lift himself into bed from his wheelchair just a few hours ago didn’t even pass through his mind. The cocktail of adrenaline, norepinephrine, the early stages of oxygen deprivation, and unbeknownst to him, dopamine had Cas’s muscles instinctually remembering all his days as a young teen flying through the forest or charging down the polyurethane faux gravel as he fought tooth and nail to win the 200-meter dash. He began to lean forwards and dig in his toes as his muscles remembered the brutal charges of his brief stint as a linebacker on his highschool football team. As he closed in, he even briefly flashed back to one of his last days of grade school as he and his friends gave up on actually playing their game of soccer and just spent the whole gym class trying to slide-tackle each other.


At the last moment, he kicked out, and with a scream of some mix of terror, anger, and who-knows-what, he sent himself into a slide underneath the rearing mass of the enormous bug.


Hammering his finger down on the trigger of the [red rocketeer mk 3] tight enough he thought he was going to break the thing, he lined the sights up with the fleshy underbelly. He wanted this thing to die. Even after everything he’d been through in his life, he wanted the thing in front of him to die more than he’d ever wanted it. It felt real. He was hammering down the trigger as much with his finger as he was with his will, and he could almost feel it somehow. No, maybe even more than some vague daydream like the rest of this shit show had been.


He could ***feel*** the energy coursing up from his chest, down his arms, into his hands, and further into the already angrily glowing barrel of the gun. But this time, it wasn’t like an incandescent lightbulb that was being overcharged, glowing like a beacon ready to burst from the overflow of voltage coursing through it. The chamber glowed a bright azure blue, crackling and pulsing with an energy that emanated out to the three rings.


The moment barely took a split second, but to Cas and anyone watching (really just Sawyer) that split second stretched out to some sort of eternal moment. As soon as the light fully suffused the rings, it pulsed and *threw* the light even further. It exploded out into three sequential rings of azure light, each slightly smaller than the one behind it and with intricate runes inside the inner and outer bands.


As the rings turned in alternating directions, everything seemed to coalesce as the energy lept from the gun in a blinding spear of crackling blue light, plunging straight into the monster’s belly and blinding Cas. From Sawyer’s perspective, it punched straight through the monster and continued on into the sky. As Cas’s eyes cleared, he could see the long azure bolt fly off into the sky very clearly even at a distance, as it had left a glowing red hole straight through the beetle’s belly at least thirty centimeters wide.


”Oh.


”My.
